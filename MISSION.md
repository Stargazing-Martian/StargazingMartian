The first astronauts to set foot on Mars will want the best map possible:

- Information on the details of their routes and destinations
- Updates on current conditions
- The data needed to complete their mission quickly and safely

Your challenge is to *create a layered, integrated view of a location or route on the Martian surface* that pulls together data from multiple NASA science missions and ***could help a human explorer plan and carry out a successful Mars-walk*** while conducting new and exciting science along the way.
