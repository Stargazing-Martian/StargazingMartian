Source and scientific limits

11 sample periods are shown; this is not the full archive or a continuous daily series. Grid spacing is 6° × 3°; zooming in does not create new measurements. Daily maps can combine observations from a 1–7 sol window.

This is not a current weather forecast or a rover's local temperature/wind measurement. Dates are not matched to the selected route. Route safety for an astronaut also needs terrain, local conditions and equipment limits to be assessed separately.

Temperature source and limits

Published NASA MGS/TES TSURF surface temperatures, averaged without weighting by Stargazing in 6° × 3° cells. Three sample date ranges are shown; only observations between 13:00 and 15:00 local solar time are used.

Grey cells are not filled in. The spread is the sample standard deviation of the observations, not measurement uncertainty. The source gives no separate measurement error for TSURF. Nearby observations may be correlated.

This is not air temperature, thermal inertia, daily minimum/maximum temperature or a live forecast. Rover/mission dates are not matched automatically; astronaut route safety also needs local conditions and equipment limits.
